About

I’m Tiwhi — a Nigerian visual artist and Creative Director specializing in photography and videography that goes beyond documentation. I create visuals that don’t just capture moments, but translate them into feeling and story.

From editorial portraits to brandworks campaigns and cinematic short films, I approach every project with a bold, intentional eye — crafting imagery that connects, elevates, and leaves a lasting impression. The camera is my language, and every frame is spoken with purpose.

"Every frame is a decision. Every cut is a statement."
